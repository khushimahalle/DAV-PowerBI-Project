# 📊 AI Job Market & Skills Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **AI Job Market & Skills Intelligence Dashboard** is a Data Analytics & Visualisation project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The project analyzes the global AI job market to understand employment trends, job roles, salary patterns, work arrangements, experience levels, and the skills demanded by organizations.

The dashboard transforms raw job-market data into interactive visualizations and meaningful insights that can help students, job seekers, recruiters, and organizations understand the changing AI job market.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze global AI job market trends.
- Identify the most in-demand AI job roles.
- Analyze salary patterns across the job market.
- Understand the demand for different AI skills.
- Compare skills required for Analytics and Engineering roles.
- Analyze job distribution based on experience level.
- Analyze job distribution based on company size and company type.
- Understand remote, hybrid, and onsite work arrangements.
- Analyze AI job trends across different countries and years.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for exploring job-market insights.

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard provides several insights into the AI job market:

- Analytics is one of the major job categories in the analyzed dataset.
- AI job demand changes across different years.
- Remote, hybrid, and onsite work arrangements have a significant presence in the AI job market.
- Python, cloud technologies, machine learning frameworks, and SQL are important skills in AI-related jobs.
- Skill requirements can differ between Analytics and Engineering roles.
- Experience level has a significant impact on the distribution of AI job opportunities.
- Companies of different sizes contribute to the overall AI job market.
- The dashboard enables country-wise and year-wise exploration of AI employment trends.
